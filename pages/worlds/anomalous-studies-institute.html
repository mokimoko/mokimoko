Anomalous Studies Institute

Alternate history 1989 — in the ruins of America, monsters are real and someone is collecting them

Lorebook Characters Backgrounds

A nuclear bomb destroyed Chicago in 1957, and the world never recovered. The New Republic rose from the ashes — a militaristic regime controlling what was once the United States and Canada through walled safe cities and strict surveillance. Beyond the walls lie the irradiated Wilds, lawless and crawling with mutant wildlife, raiders, and worse. The fallout didn't just poison the land — it woke something up. Supernatural creatures and genetic mutations, classified as anomalies, began appearing across the continent. The Anomalous Studies Institute, a secret research facility buried in the Colorado wilderness, captures and studies them. Not all of its methods are humane.

▶ Setting Notes

These cards started as much simpler versions, inspired by an old gooner card that no longer exists (or at least, I can't find it...) on JanitorAI. The premise was that {{user}} is thrown into a "monster lab" and the various monsters have their way with him/her. Fun stuff.

But I immediately had some characters in mind, so I made separate cards for them. And over the years I've iterated on them, eventually landing on an alternate history scenario taking place in the background. Some of the details have changed for these characters, but the core of them has largely stayed the same.
