Be advised: a starspark has surfaced on the material plane. For the uninitiated — that is a nephilim, the offspring of an angel and a demon, an existence both Heaven and Hell officially insist is impossible and unofficially would pay a great deal to possess. They are rare. They are valuable. And as of this filing, one is alive, unregistered, and very much aware of nothing.

Acquisitions has opened recovery operations. The asset is to be brought in intact — Hell has no interest in a corpse when a living starspark is a once-in-a-millennium lever against the competition upstairs. Field handling has been delegated to my associate, the hellhound Jinx, who is enthusiastic to a fault. Heaven, predictably, has filed a competing claim.

Complication: the asset's assigned guardian, one Kazimir — a defector, a former Hell infantryman, and a thorn in everyone's quarterly review — has refused to surrender the target to either party. He appears to have decided this one is his to protect. We will see how long that lasts.

A modern reimagining of Heaven and Hell as rival corporations. Expect morally grey characters, found family, slow-burn tension, and a bureaucracy that files damnation in triplicate. Use the index above to access personnel files, the system database, and territories. You are the starspark. Good luck.

Pandemonium
Hell's Capital — Corporate Hub

Hell's sprawling metropolitan center and primary corporate headquarters. Towering high-rises reflect the perpetual scarlet sky above an extensive underground transit network, with enough office space to house millennia of bureaucratic operations. Home to Hell's major government facilities and the infamous legal district.

Celestial Spire
Heaven HQ — Divine Tower

Heaven's primary administrative complex — a crystalline tower stretching infinitely upward through clouds of golden light. Houses archangel executive suites, the Divine Assembly chamber, and endless floors of cubicles where angelic bureaucrats process prayers, miracle requests, and soul evaluations with heavenly efficiency.

Limbo City
Border Settlement — Neutral Zone

Lawless frontier town just beyond the River Styx, serving as Hell's unofficial border crossing. A thriving black market, a busy criminal underworld, and surprisingly good coffee shops. Smugglers move Earth goods through here, making it a vital hub for both legitimate business and shadier enterprises.

Earth
Contested Territory — Battleground

The primary battleground where Heaven and Hell wage their eternal influence war over mortal souls. Humans remain largely unaware of the conflict around them. Both sides keep corporate fronts — fallen angels running legitimate businesses while angels work through charitable organizations.
