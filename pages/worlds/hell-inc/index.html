SUBJECT: Hell, Inc. Universe Documentation & Personnel Database

This central repository serves as documentation for the Hell, Inc. universe - a personal worldbuilding project exploring what happens when the afterlife operates like a modern corporation, complete with bureaucratic inefficiency, office politics, and departmental rivalries that span millennia.

The setting combines urban fantasy elements with corporate satire, following characters who navigate the absurdity of celestial bureaucracy while dealing with very real supernatural consequences. Current operational focus includes exploring the tension between individual agency and institutional expectations, the possibility of redemption in systems designed to categorize people as "good" or "evil," and what happens when a reformed demon has to work customer service for eternity.

WARNING: Expect morally complex characters, redemption arcs, found family dynamics, enemies-to-lovers tension, and probably too many jokes about filing paperwork in triplicate. Side effects may include existential questions about the nature of good and evil, an inexplicable urge to organize your own afterlife bureaucracy, and sudden sympathy for supernatural middle management.

CURRENT STATUS: Active development. New personnel files and territorial documentation added regularly.
