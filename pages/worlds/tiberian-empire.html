The Tiberian Empire

Pre-industrial imperial fantasy — scarce women, sworn oaths, and a winter that should not be coming

Overview Territories Lorebook Characters Backgrounds

A centralized empire roughly two centuries old, forged by Emperor Tiber out of the chaos of the last High Winter — and never handed back. It is a Greco-Roman imperial society filtered through medieval feudalism: no gunpowder, very little magic, and a court that runs on bloodline, oath, and leverage. Roughly three boys are born for every girl, so women are scarce and fiercely valued, every match a negotiation. All men serve their conscription, a duke's son beside a farmer's, and the army is where most have their first taste of love and loss alike. The current dynasty seized the throne a decade ago, when the Silver Knight cut down the old tyrant. Now an aging emperor, a Gallemere queen, and a crown prince nearing service age circle one another — while in the frozen north, Dunhallow's shamans swear the cycle is turning centuries too early, and no one south of the snow will listen.

▶ Setting Notes

You can probably already see this was heavily influenced by a certain popular grimdark fantasy series which may or may not ever be completed. ;P But I put my own spin on it.

I'm really interested in gender roles and how people perform gender—it's such an interesting topic. This setting explores that a little bit in that there's a massive sex imbalance, with way more men than women. In a world like that, presumably society would adapt, for better or worse.

The Six Territories

Dunhallow
The frozen north
Harsh, remote, and fiercely honor-bound. The last to bend the knee, ruled still by its old line. Its shamans keep the oldest memory — and raise the alarm no one heeds.
Kingsward
The capital, seat of Aventine
The imperial heart and the most treacherous ground in the empire. The emperor rules here directly, and at court everyone watches everyone.
Gallemere
Dealmakers and diplomats
Not the richest or strongest, but the most politically nimble. Home of the Queen and the Silver Knight, and the philosophy of cleverness over brute force.
Lissane
Trade and wealth
Ports, rivers, and agricultural riches that feed and fund the empire. Marriages here are financial mergers, and Lissane funds whoever sits the throne.
Guardia
The martial border
Loyal, practical, and built on military service. It produces career soldiers, bears the brunt of foreign wars, and quietly resents bleeding so Lissane grows fat.
Ballyrath
The forgotten coast
Poor, warm, and easygoing — the empire leaves it alone because there is nothing worth taking. The most socially relaxed corner of the realm, and tougher in a scrap than anyone expects.

Border Unit
Guardia conscripts
You've been conscripted into Tiber's army. The empire is at peace for the most part, but there are still small skirmishes along Guardia's border with Urh—and that's where your unit gets posted. It's a boring assignment in an isolated location. The soldiers are young men from all over the empire, and they spend most of their free time gambling and horsing around when they aren't running patrols or doing chores. Unfortunately for you, there's a lot of pent-up energy in service and very few outlets. Your unit has decided that you're a shared resource, and they'll take every opportunity to get you into compromising situations.
